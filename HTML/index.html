ABOUT CAMPUS COMPANION

Built to Make Student Life Easier

Campus Companion is an all-in-one student productivity platform designed to help college students stay organized, focused, and in control of their academic journey.

From managing your daily timetable and tracking attendance to calculating CGPA, organizing assignments, saving notes, and maintaining focused study sessions, Campus Companion brings everything you need into one simple and convenient dashboard.

Instead of switching between different apps and notebooks, students can manage their academic responsibilities, monitor their progress, and build better study habits all in one place.
